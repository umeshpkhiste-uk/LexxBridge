# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 24 November 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how LexxBridge ("LexxBridge", "we", "us") collects,
uses and protects information when you use the LexxBridge app and website
(together, the "Service"). LexxBridge is a practice-management tool for
advocates and lawyers to manage clients, cases, hearings, tasks, payments and
messaging.

This is also published in-app and on the web at `/legal/privacy` so it is
reachable without signing in, as required for the Google Play Store listing.

## 1. Who we are

LexxBridge is developed and operated by **LexxBridge**. Contact us at
**deepomeshcreation@gmail.com** for any privacy question or request.

## 2. Information we collect

### a) Information you provide directly
- **Account details**: name, email address, phone number, password (stored
  hashed by our authentication provider, never in plain text).
- **Professional profile**: headline, bio, practice areas, courts, city,
  profile photo, and optionally date of birth and address.
- **Client & case data**: client names, contact details, case numbers, case
  types, courts, opposite-party names, filing dates, hearing dates, tasks,
  meeting notes and fee/payment records that you enter to manage your
  practice. This data belongs to you and is private to your account by
  default.
- **Messages**: chat messages you send to other advocates on the platform.
  Messages are end-to-end encrypted — the key pair is generated and stored on
  your device, and we cannot read message content.
- **Files & photos**: documents, receipts and images you attach to cases,
  chats, posts or your profile, uploaded via your camera or photo library.
- **Support requests**: anything you send us directly by email.

### b) Information collected automatically
- **Usage & diagnostic data**: basic device information (device type,
  operating system, app version), crash and error logs, and IP address
  (captured in ordinary server/network logs for security, fraud prevention
  and troubleshooting), used to keep the Service reliable and secure.
- **Biometric authentication**: if you enable Face ID / fingerprint / Touch
  ID login, your device's operating system handles the biometric match
  locally. LexxBridge never receives, stores or transmits biometric data —
  it only receives a yes/no "authenticated" result from your device.

We do **not** collect location data and do not use advertising SDKs or
advertising identifiers.

## 3. How we use information

We use the information above to:
- Provide, operate and maintain the Service (storing your clients, cases,
  hearings, tasks and payments; delivering chat messages; sending
  notifications).
- Authenticate you and secure your account (password login, biometric /
  app-lock unlock, session management).
- Let other advocates you choose to connect with see the parts of your
  professional profile you've made visible (see "Profile visibility" in the
  app — Public, Connections only, or Private).
- Send you account-related emails (email confirmation, password reset) and,
  if enabled, in-app/push notifications for messages, tasks and hearings.
- Diagnose and fix bugs, and improve performance and reliability.
- Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, we do not use your client or case
data to train any third-party AI model, and we do not use your data —
including message content, client or case data — to build an advertising
profile or show you targeted ads.

## 4. Who we share information with

We use the following service providers (Data Processors) to run LexxBridge.
Each processes personal data only on our behalf and only for the purpose
listed:

- **Supabase** — database, sign-in and file storage. Holds all account and
  practice data you store in LexxBridge.
- **Vercel** — hosts the LexxBridge website (`lexxbridge.app`). Receives
  standard web request data such as IP address and browser type.
- **Resend** — sends our emails (sign-up confirmation, password reset,
  account notices, replies to your feedback and grievances). Receives your
  email address and the email's content.
- **Expo** — delivers push notifications to your phone, through Apple Push
  Notification service and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging. Receives your
  device's push token and the notification's title and short text — never
  your message content, which is end-to-end encrypted.
- **Google and Apple** — only if you choose "Continue with Google" or
  "Continue with Apple": they confirm your identity and share your name and
  email address with us.
- **Razorpay** — processes subscription payments on `lexxbridge.app`.
  Receives your email address and the payment details you enter at checkout
  (card, UPI or bank). We receive only your subscription's status and
  renewal date — never your full card or UPI details.
- **Other advocates you connect with**: only the profile fields you choose
  to make visible, and messages you choose to send them.
- We do not share your client, case or financial data with any other third
  party, and we do not sell personal information to anyone.
- We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights,
  property or safety of LexxBridge, our users, or the public.

**Information about your clients:** when you enter personal data about your
clients or other people (for example a client's name, phone number or case
details), you decide why and how that data is processed, so you are the
Data Fiduciary for it under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
LexxBridge processes it only on your behalf, as your Data Processor, to
provide the Service to you — we never use it for any other purpose, never
contact your clients, and erase it when you delete it or your account.
Requests from your clients about their data should be made to you.

## 5. Cookies and similar technologies

The web version of LexxBridge (`lexxbridge.app`) uses only essential local
storage (such as your session token, so you stay signed in) and no
third-party advertising or tracking cookies. We do not use cookies or
similar technologies to track you across other websites or apps.

## 6. Data security

- Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.
- Chat messages are end-to-end encrypted; the decryption key never leaves
  your device.
- Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Data at rest is encrypted by our
  backend provider.
- Row-level access controls ensure your clients, cases, hearings, tasks and
  payments are visible only to your account.
- No system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. By
  using the Service, you acknowledge and accept the inherent risk of
  unauthorized access, hacking, data breaches or data loss that can occur
  despite these safeguards — including risks arising from your own device,
  credentials or network being compromised. To the maximum extent permitted
  by applicable law, we are not liable for loss or unauthorized disclosure
  of information resulting from circumstances beyond our reasonable
  control, except where caused by our own gross negligence or willful
  misconduct, or where such liability cannot be excluded by law.
- This does not affect our legal obligations as a data fiduciary under
  India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 — including notifying
  you and the relevant authority in the event of a personal data breach, as
  required by law.

## 7. Your choices and rights

From **Profile → Settings** in the app you can, at any time:
- **Export your data** — download a full copy of everything associated with
  your account.
- **Delete your account** — permanently erase your profile, clients, cases,
  hearings, documents, payments, posts, chats, connections, notifications
  and every uploaded file. This cannot be undone.
- **Change your profile visibility** (Public / Connections only / Private).
- **Turn off notifications**.

If you can't access the app, see [`/legal/delete-account`](./ACCOUNT_DELETION.md)
for how to request deletion by email instead.

As a Data Principal under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act,
2023, you have the right to obtain a summary of the personal data we hold
about you and how it's processed, to have inaccurate or incomplete data
corrected and updated, to have your personal data erased, and to a
readily-available means of grievance redressal (see "Grievance Officer"
below). Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights (e.g.
under GDPR or the CCPA) to access, correct, port or restrict processing of
your personal data. Contact us at **deepomeshcreation@gmail.com** to
exercise any of these rights.

**Withdrawing consent:** you can withdraw your consent at any time from
**Settings → Privacy & your rights → Withdraw consent**, as easily as you
gave it. Because LexxBridge cannot provide any part of the Service without
processing your personal data, withdrawing consent closes your account and
permanently erases your data, as described above. Withdrawal does not affect
processing that happened before it.

**Nominating someone:** you can nominate a person to exercise these rights on
your behalf if you die or become incapacitated, from **Settings → Privacy &
your rights → Nominee**. Your nominee makes a request by writing to the
Grievance Officer with supporting proof, which we verify against the details
you saved.

**Complaining to the Data Protection Board of India:** if you are not
satisfied with how we handle a grievance, you may file a complaint with the
Data Protection Board of India, which accepts complaints online through its
digital platform. The Act requires you to first raise the grievance with us
(see "Grievance Officer" below) and give us the chance to respond.

## 8. Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your
account, your data is permanently removed from our production systems,
subject to any residual copies in encrypted backups, which are purged on
our normal backup rotation schedule.

## 9. Children's privacy

LexxBridge is intended for licensed advocates, lawyers and legal
professionals and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect
information from anyone under 18.

## 10. International transfers

Your information may be processed in a country other than the one you live
in. We take steps to ensure it receives an adequate level of protection
wherever it is processed.
