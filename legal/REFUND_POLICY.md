# Refund & Cancellation Policy

**Last updated: 24 November 2026**

This policy covers the LexxBridge monthly subscription. It forms part of our
[Terms of Service](./TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md). Published in the app and at
`https://lexxbridge.app/legal/refund`.

## 1. Free trial

New accounts get 7 days with every feature unlocked. No card or UPI details
are needed, and nothing is charged when the trial ends — unless you choose
to subscribe, your account simply moves to the free plan (up to 5 clients).

## 2. The monthly plan

The monthly plan is billed in advance, once a month from the day you
subscribe, and renews automatically until you cancel. Payments are processed
by Razorpay using the UPI Autopay mandate or card you authorise. The price is
shown on the Plan & Subscription page before you pay, in Indian Rupees and
including applicable taxes unless stated otherwise.

## 3. Cancelling

You can cancel at any time from Profile → Plan & Subscription, on
lexxbridge.app or in the app. Cancelling takes effect immediately: you won't
be charged again, and everything stays unlocked until the end of the month
you've already paid for. After that your account moves to the free plan.
Nothing is deleted.

Revoking the Autopay mandate from your UPI or bank app also stops future
charges; your account then moves to the free plan at the end of the paid
month.

## 4. Refunds

Because you can cancel at any time and keep access until the end of the paid
month, payments for a month that has already started are not refunded,
including for days you didn't use.

We will refund a payment in full if:

- you were charged more than once for the same month;
- you were charged after your cancellation took effect;
- you were charged because of a technical error on our side; or
- the paid features were unavailable for most of the month because of a
  fault on our side.

To request a refund, email **deepomeshcreation@gmail.com** within 30 days of
the charge, from your account's email address, with the date and amount (and
the Razorpay payment ID from your receipt, if you have it). We'll reply
within 7 working days. Approved refunds go back to the original payment
method; your bank or card issuer usually takes 5–7 working days to show it.

## 5. Failed payments

If a monthly payment fails, Razorpay may retry it over the next few days. If
it still can't be collected, your account moves to the free plan. Your data
stays, and you can subscribe again at any time.

## 6. Deleting your account

Deleting your account cancels your subscription automatically, so you won't
be charged again. The rest of the month already paid for is not refunded.

## 7. Price changes

We'll email you at least 30 days before changing the price of your plan. The
new price applies only from your next billing month after that notice, and
you can cancel before then.
